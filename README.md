# Snake Sprint

A lightweight browser game built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Play locally

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Press **Start** (or **Space**) to begin.
3. Move with **Arrow keys** or **WASD**.
4. Press **R** to restart.

## Rules

- Eat red food to score points and grow longer.
- Every 5 points, the snake speeds up.
- Hitting the wall or your own body ends the game.
- Your best score is saved in `localStorage`.
